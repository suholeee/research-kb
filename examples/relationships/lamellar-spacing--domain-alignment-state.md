# Lamellar spacing ↔ domain alignment state

## Variables
- [[lamellar-spacing|Lamellar spacing]]
- Domain alignment state: aligned vs misaligned

## Relationship
- Reduced lamellar spacing promotes out-of-plane domain alignment, while larger spacing is associated with misalignment.
- [Lee_JACS_2024](../notes/Lee_JACS_2024.md) shows that lowering `D` by `CaCl2`, `NaCl`, or `PEG 10K` can induce alignment and growth, supporting distance rather than Ca2+-specific chemistry as the controlling factor.
- The same note reports that MD simulations favor aligned states only at the smaller spacing (`D = 70 Å`) and not at the larger spacing (`D = 105 Å`).

## Evidence
- [Lee_JACS_2024](../notes/Lee_JACS_2024.md)
- [[lamellar-spacing|Lamellar spacing]]
- [[x-ray-reflectivity|X-ray reflectivity]]

## Interpretation
- In the current membrane papers, lamellar spacing behaves as a control parameter for interlayer domain registration.

## Caveats
- The present evidence base is concentrated in one experimental and simulation study.
- The mechanism is interpreted through overlapping surface-water regions and hydrogen-bond penalties, but that mechanistic layer is still system-specific.

## Confidence
- High
